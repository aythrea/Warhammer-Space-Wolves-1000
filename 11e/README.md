# Why I Don't Play Warhammer 40,000 Anymore

## Executive Summary — Space Wolves

I previously owned **10,000+ points of Space Wolves** and primarily played low-point escalation leagues. My preferred army wasn't generic Space Marines with Space Wolves flavor; it was a **hyper-aggressive, melee-focused cavalry/beast army** whose lore, units, keywords, and rules all reinforced one another.

Two of my surviving 9th-edition lists establish the benchmark:

- **555 points:** Harald Deathwolf + 6 Thunderwolf Cavalry + 15 Fenrisian Wolves
- **996 points:** Harald Deathwolf + Logan Grimnar on Stormrider + 9 Thunderwolf Cavalry + 13 Fenrisian Wolves

Both contained **zero generic Space Marines**. The smaller army naturally escalated into the larger one without changing its identity.

Current Warhammer 40,000 retains Thunderwolf Cavalry and Fenrisian Wolves, but much of the supporting ecosystem that made this style of army work has disappeared from current matched play. Harald Deathwolf and the mounted Wolf Lord/Battle Leader roles are gone, while Logan Grimnar on Stormrider is Legends. Other distinctive pieces of my collection, including Long Fangs and the Stormwolf/Stormfang, have also moved to Legends.

Since the escalation leagues I play in don't allow Legends, that distinction matters.

## My Return-to-40K Test

> **Can current Space Wolves support a self-contained, melee-first Thunderwolf/Fenrisian Wolf army from roughly 500–1,250 points, with appropriate mounted leadership and meaningful faction synergy, without relying on Legends or generic Space Marines to fill the holes?**

If **yes**, then it's worth investigating a return and considering the cost of rebuilding.

If **no**, I'm content to remain retired rather than spend money trying to recreate the experience through generic Primaris units.

This isn't primarily about whether Space Wolves are competitive, whether Thunderwolf Cavalry have a current datasheet, or whether a legal army can still be assembled under the Space Wolves name.

It's about whether the rules still support the **army identity I actually played**.

## The Collection

I also still have unopened Space Wolves kits in my pile of shame, including kits that have subsequently become discontinued or moved to Legends.

For now, those stay sealed.

Before rebuilding anything, they need to be inventoried and evaluated individually as models to **build, convert, preserve, sell, or trade**.

Until the Space Wolves rules can pass the test above, there's little reason to crack open old kits or spend hundreds of dollars building generic Primaris Marines simply because the rules allow me to paint them Fenrisian grey.
